Current research

Coupled control of a robot arm and a long flexible truss

Master's thesis at the School of Aerospace Engineering, BIT, supervised by Prof. Haiyan Hu and Prof. Kai Luo. Robots increasingly work on long, light structures, in orbit and on the ground. Such structures deflect strongly and vibrate slowly, and when the arm moves, the structure moves the arm back. Above about ten percent deflection the usual linear beam model is no longer accurate, so the controller needs a model that handles large rotation.

Model

Absolute nodal coordinate formulation (ANCF) for the truss, coupled with the arm's rigid-body dynamics, in 2D and in 3D with torsion. Verified against theory and an independent multibody code.

Estimate

Extended Kalman filter that reconstructs the full truss state from motion-capture markers and joint encoders, with sensor delay handled exactly.

Control

Nonlinear model predictive control with real-time iteration, compiled and split across processes to meet the sampling deadline, with a ROS 2 interface for the lab platform.

Thesis work

What I built for the thesis so far

Each piece below is a working, tested part of the same simulation framework, written in Python.

Design

Truss design study

A 3D space-frame finite element model of a printed lattice truss: longerons, battens and diagonals as 3D frame elements. It gives natural frequencies, mode types and the equivalent bending and torsion stiffness used by the beam model, and showed how open sections trade bending for twist.

Modelling

Large-deformation beam model

Planar ANCF beam with exact strain and curvature, an analytic tangent and generalized-α time integration. Checked against Euler–Bernoulli frequencies, the exact elastica for large static deflection, and energy conservation.

Validation

Independent plant in Exudyn

The same truss and arm rebuilt in the Exudyn multibody code and used as the plant, so the controller is never tested against its own model.

Estimation

State estimation from markers

Extended Kalman filter that recovers the full truss state from motion-capture markers on the tip, with the camera delay handled exactly, and hybrid identification of stiffness, payload and damping.

Control

Fixed and sliding arm MPC

Linear and ANCF-based model predictive control for an arm fixed at the tip and for an arm sliding along the truss on a carriage, compared over repeated noisy runs.

3D

Bending and torsion in 3D

3D ANCF beam with an elliptic section and an offset arm, so arm motion also twists the truss; a modal Kalman filter and a 3D MPC that uses the bending–torsion coupling.

Data-driven

Data-driven prediction models

Hankel dynamic mode decomposition with control (DMDc) as an alternative prediction model, compared with the physics-based one inside the same MPC.

Real time

Real-time controller and ROS 2 node

Compiled with Numba and split across processes so estimation and control meet their deadlines, tested against the wall clock in software-in-the-loop runs, with a ROS 2 node ready for the lab platform.

MATLAB · Simulink · GNC

Spacecraft GNC: attitude control and autonomous docking

A full guidance, navigation and control simulation in MATLAB/Simulink.

  • Nonlinear attitude dynamics with gravity-gradient, aerodynamic, solar-pressure (with eclipse) and magnetic disturbance torques
  • Gyroscope and star-tracker models with bias and noise, fused with a Kalman filter

Literature review · in preparation

Reduced-order models for controlling flexible multibody systems

A review comparing model-reduction methods by what a controller needs: cost per step, accuracy at large deformation, an observable state and an error bound. From component modes to spectral submanifolds and data-driven models.
